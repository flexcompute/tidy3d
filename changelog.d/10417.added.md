`ElectroOpticPerturbation` changes the refractive index of a perturbation medium through the Pockels and DC Kerr effects, given a static electric field in `V/um` to `perturbed_mediums_copy(electric_field=...)`, such as the field of a charge simulation. Pockels coefficients are in `um/V` and Kerr coefficients in `um^2/V^2`. Only the diagonal of the impermeability change in simulation axes is applied. The index change of each diagonal component uses its unperturbed index at the model's `freq` and is applied at all frequencies. `PerturbationAnisotropicMedium` is the perturbation medium for anisotropic crystals.
