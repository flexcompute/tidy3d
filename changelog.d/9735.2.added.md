Added `SimulationData.specific_absorption_rate(monitor_name)` and `SimulationData.specific_absorption_rate_from_densities(monitor_name, density)`, the point specific absorption rate `[W/kg]` from a `FieldStructureMonitor`: the absorbed power density divided by the mass density of each sample's owning medium, taken from the medium's heat medium or given as a mapping from medium to density in kg/um^3; 2D materials, lumped elements and automatic PEC frames contribute zero, and any other medium that absorbs power in the monitor region without a density raises an error.
