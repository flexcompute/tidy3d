`Simulation.to_gdstk`, `Simulation.to_gds` and `Simulation.to_gds_file` now cut structures that are infinite in the exported plane (for example an infinite substrate), including structures with a custom medium, at the simulation bounds. Previously the polygons with infinite coordinates were passed to the `gdstk` boolean operation, which does not handle non-finite coordinates: depending on the coordinates and the platform, the structure was silently missing from the export or the process aborted, and a structure with a custom medium that is infinite in the plane could not be exported.
