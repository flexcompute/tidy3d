Fixed EME mode matching and backward propagation in cells whose permittivity tensor is not diagonal in the cell frame: a non-diagonal `FullyAnisotropicMedium`, or anisotropic media in bends with `bend_medium_frame="global"`. Normalized port modes and `EMEModeSolverMonitor` modes of such cells are normalized by the flux that normalizes the S-matrix, so that for lossy modes they remain the basis the S-matrix is expressed in.
