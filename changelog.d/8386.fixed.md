`Structure.eps_diagonal`, `Structure.eps_comp`, and therefore `Simulation.epsilon`, `Simulation.epsilon_on_grid`, and `ModeSimulation.run_local` now use the optical portion of a `MultiPhysicsMedium`, so structures no longer need to be rewritten with their bare optical media. Under `structure_priority_mode='conductor'`, a `MultiPhysicsMedium` wrapping a `LossyMetalMedium` or `PECMedium` receives the documented conductor priority, so an overlapping dielectric does not overwrite it. Optical queries on a `MultiPhysicsMedium` with no `optical` medium raise an error naming the missing field, and a `MultiPhysicsMedium` wrapping a `Medium2D` is rejected at validation.
