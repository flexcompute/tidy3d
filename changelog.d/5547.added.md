`LayerRefinementSpec` now refines axis-unaligned in-plane metal edges (`in_plane_edge_refinement`, default `"mirror_corner"`) and resolves small disjoint metal geometries such as vias (`min_grids_per_geometry`, default `2`), with a tunable `CornerFinderSpec.axis_aligned_angle_threshold`; both are on by default and give existing setups a finer mesh, set them to `None` to keep the previous mesh.
