Added `ModeStructureMonitor` and `ModeStructureData`, the mode-solver producer of the matched electric-field, permittivity, and structure-ownership primitives that `FieldStructureMonitor` records for FDTD, together with `ModeSimulationData.field_structure(...)`, `optical_generation(...)` and `per_component_optical_generation(...)`. The monitor takes a planar box on the mode plane and records the permittivity at every `ModeSimulation.freqs` plus the per-Yee structure ownership as `ModeStructureData`; `field_structure(...)` pairs that record with the electric field of the ordinary mode result, selected by `mode_index` and `freq`, and returns the matched set as `ModeFieldStructureData`, on which the same optical-generation kernel runs as for the FDTD sibling. Because the pairing only holds while the field sits at the same Yee positions as the recorded permittivity, a `ModeSimulation` carrying the monitor must set `colocate=False` and keep `Ex`, `Ey` and `Ez` in `fields`.
