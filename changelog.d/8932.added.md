`SolidMedium.conductivity` and `SolidMedium.capacity` accept temperature-dependent models in heat simulations, including those with a conduction heat source: `PowerLawConductivity`, `PolynomialConductivity` and `InversePolynomialConductivity` (with a scalar or `AnisotropicConductivity` reference), and `PowerLawHeatCapacity` and `PolynomialHeatCapacity`, each clamped to its `temperature_range`.
`InversePolynomialConductivity.from_resistivity` builds the model from a thermal-resistivity fit `w0 + w1 T + ...` in powers of `T`, the form such fits are usually published in.
