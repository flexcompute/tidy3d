A `SurfaceFieldStructureMonitor` and its `SurfaceFieldStructureData` record the H field (required, since the surface loss is computed from it) and optionally the E field, the interface normal, and per-vertex structure-ownership data on a triangulated PEC/lossy-metal surface, exposing derived `h_tan_squared` and `surface_loss_density` (`0.5 * Re(Z_s) * |H_tan|^2`, `Re(Z_s)` looked up from the owning structure's medium), and correctly expand under simulation symmetry. `SimulationData.per_structure_loss(volumetric_monitor_name=..., surface_monitor_name=...)` returns the total electromagnetic power loss `[W]` per structure in 3D simulations as a `StructureLossDataArray` over `f` and `structure_index`, taking each structure's loss from the monitor that records its kind: the surface loss of PEC and of lossy metals under the default surface impedance subpixel method from a `SurfaceFieldStructureMonitor`, the volumetric loss of all other media from a `FieldStructureMonitor` (either or both; both must record the same frequencies; only structures sampled by a supplied monitor are listed, and NaN for one its own monitor did not sample).
