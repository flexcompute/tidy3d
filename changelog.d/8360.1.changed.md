On EME results that store `port_modes_backward`, `smatrix_in_basis` now requires a pair of bases recorded with `direction="+"` and `direction="-"` for each changed port, and `field_in_basis` takes either a single basis recorded in the direction of the excitation or such a pair. These directional basis changes require `tidy3d-extras` with an EME license. On other EME results, `smatrix_in_basis` and `field_in_basis` now reject a single basis recorded with `direction="-"`, which the ports are not expressed in. Local EME (`mode_simulations`, `propagate`) now rejects cells with a non-diagonal `FullyAnisotropicMedium`; run such simulations on the server. Local EME staging and propagation also reject supplied mode data recorded with `direction="-"`; provide forward (`'+'`) mode fields.
