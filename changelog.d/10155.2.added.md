Hosted charge simulations that use `ProtectedDopingRef` need a voltage sweep with at least two points, run on the accelerated charge solver (`use_accelerated_solver=False` is rejected), and accept only a `SteadyCapacitanceMonitor` that covers the whole simulation; the solver log is replaced by a notice and the residual and CFL traces are not returned. The real doping box has the center and size of the reference.
