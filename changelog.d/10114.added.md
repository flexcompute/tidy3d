Added incomplete ionization of dopants to the accelerated charge solver: `SemiconductorMedium.donor_ionization` and `acceptor_ionization` take a `PearsonBardeenIonization` (a level whose depth can fall with the doping), an `AltermattSchenkIonization` (silicon, fully ionized above the Mott transition) or a `MultiSiteIonization` (a dopant on inequivalent lattice sites), and the new `SteadyDopingMonitor` records the donor and acceptor concentrations and, when a law is set, the ionized ones. With a law, `SteadyCapacitanceMonitor` and `DeviceCharacteristics.steady_dc_electron_capacitance` / `steady_dc_hole_capacitance` also count the carriers bound on dopants, while media without a law are unchanged. With incomplete ionization, `SlotboomBandGapNarrowing` is shifted so that the equilibrium minority-carrier density stays the one it was fitted to.
