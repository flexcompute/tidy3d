Added second-order subpixel-smoothed projection (SSP2) through `smoothed_projection(..., order=2)` and `projection="ssp2"` in `FilterAndProject`, `make_filter_and_project`, and the inverse-design plugin's `FilterProject`. The shared filter-and-project pipeline also supports SSP1 with `projection="ssp1"`. SSP supports periodic derivatives with `padding="wrap"`; the design plugin validates two-dimensional grids with equal physical cell spacing.
