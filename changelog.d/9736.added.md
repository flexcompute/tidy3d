Added `FieldStructureTimeMonitor` and `FieldStructureTimeData`, the time-domain counterpart of `FieldStructureMonitor`: the monitor records the electric field over time together with the permittivity and conductivity the time stepping applies to each component and the per-Yee structure ownership, and its data provides the loss current density `Jx_loss`/`Jy_loss`/`Jz_loss` (`sigma * E(t)`) and the loss power density `loss_power_density` (`sum_i sigma_ii E_i(t)^2`). Given this monitor, `SimulationData.per_structure_loss(volumetric_monitor_name=...)` returns the instantaneous loss per structure as a `StructureLossTimeDataArray` over time. Only loss through a static conductivity is recorded, so most of an RF model's loss is not: a dispersive medium (including the RF material library's dielectrics and loss-tangent fits) with loss or gain in the simulation band, and a nonlinear medium, log a warning, and `per_structure_loss` reports NaN for them; a lossless dispersive medium is recorded exactly; a fully anisotropic or time-modulated medium reaching the monitor is rejected. `SimulationData.per_structure_loss` warns when it reports NaN for a lossy metal under the surface impedance method because no `SurfaceFieldStructureMonitor` was given, which is always the case in the time domain.
