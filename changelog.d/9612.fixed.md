`HeatChargeSimulation.plot` and `plot_boundaries` no longer draw a `MediumMediumInterface` boundary condition on faces where its two media do not meet, such as the buried interface under a layer that sits on an earlier-listed substrate. They also no longer raise an `AttributeError` when a structure joins a multi-part region of its own medium (for example an air trench opened from the background), and they now draw the condition around holes in a structure, such as a void cut into a layer. Simulation setup and results are unaffected; only the plotted boundary conditions change.
