Small-signal analysis of a charge simulation with `DistributedGeneration` now also returns the photocurrent response to a small modulation of the generation, `DeviceCharacteristics.ac_photocurrent_voltage`, read at the contact of the `SSACVoltageSource` per unit modulation depth. It is the short-circuit photocurrent, which gives the transit-limited bandwidth and the responsivity of a photodetector versus frequency. Combined with `ac_current_voltage`, it also gives the RC limit of a load. Requires the accelerated charge solver.
