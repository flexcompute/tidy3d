Small-signal analysis of a charge simulation now also returns the admittance `DeviceCharacteristics.ac_admittance_voltage` and the capacitance `DeviceCharacteristics.ac_capacitance_voltage` at the contact of the `SSACVoltageSource`, versus DC bias and frequency, so they no longer have to be derived from `ac_current_voltage` and the source amplitude. Requires the accelerated charge solver.
