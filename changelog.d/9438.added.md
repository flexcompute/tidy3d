Added `AnisotropicPermittivity` for the static `permittivity` of charge media, given by principal values and an optional rotation, or built with `from_components` from the six tensor components (accelerated charge solver only; conduction simulations still take a scalar). For a permittivity that is strongly anisotropic within the simulated cross-section, `linear_solver="direct"` is recommended, since the iterative solver may not converge.
