`CurrentBC` now accepts `model="schottky_mott"` for a current-driven Schottky contact, may share its placement with a `SurfaceRecombinationBC` on an ohmic contact, may reach a heterojunction interface when it is ohmic and shares its placement with no `SurfaceRecombinationBC`, and may be combined with small-signal analysis, which treats the current-driven contact as an open circuit. A floating contact (zero current, the only contact on its semiconductor regions) must be the only current-driven contact and cannot be combined with small-signal analysis.
