Fixed the maximum mobility `mu` of the electron `CaugheyThomasMobility` (`mobility_n`) in the `Si_MultiPhysics` variant of the `cSi` material library entry and in the documentation examples: it is now 1417 cm²/V/s, the value of Masetti et al. (1983), which is also the source of its `mu_min`, `ref_N` and `exp_N`. Simulations that use this variant get an electron mobility up to 3.7% lower, the full amount in lightly doped silicon, while hole mobility is unchanged. Setting `mu=1471.0` in `mobility_n` reproduces the previous results.
