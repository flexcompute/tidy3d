In a non-isothermal charge simulation, every solid, including a solid background `medium`, must now define a `charge` medium. A solid with heat properties only, such as `MultiPhysicsMedium(heat=SolidMedium(...))` or `Medium(heat_spec=SolidSpec(...))`, is rejected at validation and should be declared with `charge=ChargeInsulatorMedium(permittivity=...)`. Previously such a simulation failed when the solver started. Isothermal charge simulations are unaffected.
